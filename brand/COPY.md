# FinanceMeta Copy System

## Voice

FinanceMeta sounds specific, rigorous, young without being childish, and ambitious without pretending work is complete. Use short sentences, concrete nouns, and active verbs. Distinguish what exists from what is planned.

## Product vocabulary

- FinanceMeta
- Axiom Pathways
- FinTech Studio
- FinanceMeta Labs
- Global Chapters
- The Debrief
- Challenges and Competitions
- member portal
- program family
- reviewable output
- operating record
- evidence

## Claim rules

- Say `in development` until a program has a named lead, operating record, and reviewable output.
- Say `student-led` as an operating intention, not proof of reach or outcomes.
- Do not claim global presence because Global Chapters is a planned program family.
- Do not describe synthetic research runs as market evidence.
- Never invent users, partners, testimonials, placements, returns, or scale.

## Banned marketing phrases

- `unlock`
- `unleash`
- `reimagine`
- `revolutionize`
- `supercharge`
- `empower`
- `elevate`
- `seamless`
- `cutting-edge`
- `next-generation`
- `game-changing`
- `future of`
- `built for the future`
- `transform the way`
- `where finance meets`
- `everything you need`
- `whether you're`
- `not just`

## Banned filler headings

- Why choose us
- Our solutions
- Powerful features
- Built for you
- Endless possibilities

## Punctuation

- Do not use em dash characters in visible copy.
- Avoid exclamation marks.
- Prefer a period or colon when separating thoughts.

## Calls to action

Use literal destinations or actions:

- Explore programs
- Open member portal
- Apply to FinanceMeta
- Discuss a partnership

Avoid vague labels such as `Learn more`, `Get started`, or `Discover` when a more exact action exists.
