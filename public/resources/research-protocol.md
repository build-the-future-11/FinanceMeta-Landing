# FinanceMeta research protocol worksheet

Working template, September 2026. Adapt and review before use.

1. Question: What precise claim is being tested?
2. Decision: What outcome would change your interpretation?
3. Literature: What is already known and what is the gap?
4. Hypothesis: What is the falsifiable prediction?
5. Data: Source, rights, identity/hash, as-of dates, population, and exclusions.
6. Baseline: Strong simple alternative, with equal access to information.
7. Evaluation: Frozen splits, metrics, uncertainty unit, and decision rule.
8. Leakage review: Features, labels, preprocessing, tuning, and revisions.
9. Realism: Transaction costs, delays, capacity, and constraints if relevant.
10. Robustness: Prespecified sensitivity checks and comparison budget.
11. Reproduction: Runtime, dependencies, seeds, config, and exact command.
12. Reporting: Results, negative findings, failed runs, limitations, and next test.

A filled worksheet is a plan; it is not certification or independent review.
