# Before you publish a finding

FinanceMeta working checklist, September 2026.

- State the unit, population, period, and exact claim.
- Bind each number to a retained artifact and source version.
- Explain missing data, exclusions, and any repeated observations.
- Separate exploratory analysis from a prespecified test.
- Report the baseline, uncertainty, and sensitivity to assumptions.
- Keep negative, null, and inconclusive outcomes visible.
- Explain how timing, leakage, and costs were handled.
- Include instructions and rights needed to reproduce the result.
- Distinguish synthetic, historical, paper-trading, and realized evidence.
- State what the evidence cannot establish.
- Record review status accurately and provide a corrections contact.

This checklist is research guidance, not a claim of peer review.
