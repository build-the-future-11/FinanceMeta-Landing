# Review operations

Owner: Ryan. Assistant: completeness checks, organization and draft review notes when asked to process submissions. Final participation and publication decisions belong to Ryan. No autonomous outreach, admission or publication is enabled by this document. A response-time promise is not set.

## Queue
Keep general contributions, journal manuscripts and forum preprints separate. Use the provider submission ID as the stable receipt identifier; do not copy manuscript text or contact details into public analytics. Record received time, route/form version, QA flag, assigned reviewer, status, last action and next action in an access-controlled review workspace.

Statuses: received → completeness check → owner review → clarification requested / next-step offered / declined / withdrawn. Journal editorial assessment, peer review and publication permission are separate stages. Forum moderation is not peer review.

## Completeness and fit
General: readable contact channel, a contribution or learning interest, relevant preparation and availability. No prestigious affiliation requirement. Journal: accessible manuscript, question, methods/evidence, limitations, authorship, permissions and disclosures. Forum: readable working manuscript, version, authors' sharing permission and clear preprint status. A missing field requests clarification; it is not an automatic rejection.

## Confirmations and follow-up
Keep the provider's on-screen receipt. Verify respondent confirmation delivery before advertising email receipts. Use concise owner-reviewed messages that identify the form, submission and next action; do not promise a place, mentoring schedule, deadline or DOI without a recorded commitment. Ask for necessary missing material only. Publication and participation permissions remain explicit.

## Activation
For an accepted contributor, agree one bounded first task and its reviewer. Record completion only against an inspectable artifact or actual session. Report received, suitable, accepted and activated counts separately. Rejected, withdrawn, spam and QA records remain distinguishable. Do not erase failed outcomes to improve the conversion rate.
